Fuse Fiesta
Fireworks chain puzzle

One match. One spark. The whole sky lit up.

Fuse Fiesta is a fireworks puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Every rocket fires a spark the way it points, and that spark lights the first firework it reaches, which fires its own spark, and so on down the line. Tap the right rocket and a single match sets off the whole board. Tap the wrong one and the chain fizzles out early.

Hundreds of levels, every one checked to be solvable with the matches it gives you. The puzzle is the order: clear the way for a long shot, shatter the glass before it swallows your best spark, bounce sparks off mirrors. Crackers fire both ways, star shells burst in all four directions, and crates stop everything cold. Hard levels and bosses are marked before you start. Three boosters for tight spots, and a daily reward for coming back.

For iPhone and iPad, now in Australia, New Zealand and Canada. Free to play, with optional purchases and ads.
